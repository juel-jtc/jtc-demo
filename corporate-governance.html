Philosophy

Governance built
on fairness

JTC is committed to implementing the applicable principles of governance practices issued by the Capital Markets Authority for companies listed on Boursa Kuwait. Our goal is to ensure fairness for every stakeholder – our investors, customers, vendors, the community, and the governments of the countries in which we operate. We believe in sustainable corporate growth that emanates from the top leadership down through the organization to the various stakeholders. It is a reflection of our policies, our relationship with stakeholders, and our commitment to core values.

The company’s board of directors and executive Management are fully committed to ensuring the highest standards of corporate governance structure through the policies, rules, and regulations that govern its business. This framework enables the board of JTC to define the responsibilities, tasks, and duties of each member of its management, committees, and employees.
