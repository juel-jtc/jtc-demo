Ethics & Compliance

Our commitment to integrity begins in our compliance to the laws, rules and regulations that govern our business dealings. Continued growth, prosperity, and profitability are directly linked with our employee’s abilities in making decisions that are consistent with JTC’s core values and ethical principles. We at JTC pride ourselves on the below:

  • Passion for Excellence: we strive to achieve the best in everything we do.
  • Integrity: We hold ourselves to the highest ethical standards. Our interactions are open, honest and are guided by what is right.
  • Teamwork: We work together with trust, openness, honesty, and respect.
  • Inspiration: It is our aim to build a culture where people are motivated, engaged, valued, and growing.
  • Respect for Privacy: We respect each other’s privacy and pay attention to all details from the beginning to the end when handling personal information.

Our core values support and guide our leadership in establishing the strategic direction of the Company. Our employees and representatives are expected to conduct their business in accordance with these values and with our Standards of Business Ethics and Conduct (“Our Standards”). We strive to be compliant with laws, regulations, and policies; and we endeavor to conduct ourselves in a manner beyond reproach as we firmly believe that actions speak louder than words.
