Company History

From an inland transportation company founded in Kuwait in 1979 to one of the GCC's leading providers of ports management, logistics, warehousing, equipment leasing and power rental — every milestone below is taken from JTC's official company history.

1979 — Founded as an inland transportation company in Kuwait

Acquired Ports Services license for Shuwaikh Port

Launched Equipment Rental in Kuwait

Introduced Equipment Rental Operations in Qatar

Launched Power Rental in Kuwait

Established a formal subsidiary in Qatar — Road Junction Transport & Equipment Co. (RJTC)

Established a formal subsidiary in KSA — JTC Logistics Co.

Expanded Heavy Lift Capabilities

60% stake acquired by KIPCO Group

Commenced operations in Shuaiba Port for aggregate and general cargo stevedoring

Listed on the Kuwait Stock Exchange

Commissioned 10K temperature-controlled warehouses in Mina Abdullah

Opened Riyadh Depot for KSA Power Rental

Legal name changed to JTC Logistics Transportation & Stevedoring Co. K.S.C.P.; commissioned additional 10K & 12K temperature-controlled warehouses in Mina Abdullah

Opened Jeddah Depot for KSA Power Rental
