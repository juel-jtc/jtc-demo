Overview

One-stop logistics,
turnkey delivery

JTC's Contract Logistics division provides affordable, one-stop logistics services for your turnkey transportation and storage requirements in Kuwait; by offering superior service, innovation, and optimized resources. We have specialized, highly skilled workforce and are comprehensively equipped with wide range of well-maintained transportation equipment.

Land Transport

General Transportation

We provide flexible and efficient land transport solutions. Whether you need turnkey project logistics, general transportation, or fuel distribution, JTC will deliver reliable and cost-effective solutions to support our customers.

  • Flatbed & Lowbed Trailers: Standard Flatbed and high-capacity Lowbed trailers with a comprehensive range of bracing and lashing accessories
  • Maintenance Program: Effective preventive maintenance programs and emergency recovery capabilities
Projects

Project Logistics

JTC delivers optimized and reliable project logistics solutions to support client-specific requirements. Whatever the complexity and scope, we design, execute, and monitor the logistics chain strategy to help keep our promises to our loyal customers.

Bulk

Bulk Transportation

JTC is a major bulk fuel transporter since 1979, we are experts in transporting various petroleum grades. We operate and maintain KNPC approved fuel tankers in Kuwait with a wide range of capacity. In addition, our qualified and experienced drivers are regularly assessed and trained to meet stringent standards.

We offer:

  • Oil and Gas industry specification tankers
  • Bulk fuel tankers (5,000 to 36,000 liters)
  • Potable water tankers (30,000 to 40,000 liters)
  • Industrial effluent tankers (36,000 to 54,000 liters)
  • Tipper trucks (up to 32 CBM)
  • Vacuum tankers (up to 7,500 gallons)
Customs

Customs Clearance Support Services

  • Legalization / Attestation of documents, exemptions from Ministries & Authorities, support for special licenses & permits
  • Temporary import / export
Integrated

Turnkey Solutions

JTC's Contract Logistics division in conjunction with other JTC divisions, delivers integrated and turnkey solutions, making us a preferred service provider for our customers. Using our modern fleet of cranes, material handling equipment, trucks & trailers, coupled with storage facilities and value-added services, we provide complete delivery solutions from port to your end destination in Kuwait.
