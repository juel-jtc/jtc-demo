Ports Management

Overview

Single-stop turnkey
port services

With over four decades of experience, JTC Ports Management division operates at both Shuwaikh and Shuaiba ports in Kuwait offering single-stop turnkey services for cargo handling from ship to storage site.

Stevedoring

Stevedoring

We use modern equipment and well-trained personnel to ensure precise, efficient, and smooth cargo handling operations.

Services include:

  • Container Handling
  • RORO
  • Heavy Lift
  • Break Bulk Vessels
  • Aggregate Cargo Handling
Cargo Handling

Cargo Handling

The efficient and expedient handling of LCL, FCL, FTL, and various types of cargo is achieved by utilizing the latest equipment, supported by trained and experienced specialists and advanced information systems.

We handle:

  • Container Terminal: Loading, Unloading, and Stacking of OOG, Hazardous and/or Reefer Containers
  • General Cargo: Cement, Pipes, Coils, Loose Cargo, Steel, and Timber
Transportation

Transportation

Through our modern fleet of vehicles, we offer you quick and seamless transportation solutions:

  • Between port and your storage facilities
  • Shuttling of Containers and General Cargo
Reefer Facility

Reefer Plugging Facility

JTC offers 180 Power Line and 465 Genset connections along with PTI facilities for short and long-term storage of your 20ft' and 40ft' reefer container in Shuwaikh.

Our premises offer quick and easy turnaround space and ample parking for your containers. We guarantee a superior level of service and storage conditions.
