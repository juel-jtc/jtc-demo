Who we are

One of the leading service
providers in the GCC

JTC Logistics Transportation & Stevedoring Co. K.S.C.P (JTC) a Kuwaiti company founded in 1979 is a public shareholding company listed on the Kuwait Stock Exchange (KSE). Over the years, the company has undergone significant change. JTC today is a dynamic, innovative, and highly responsive customer-focused organization. With offices in Kuwait, Saudi Arabia & Qatar, we work with clients in various sectors in the GCC providing professional, high-quality, and cost-effective solutions through our operating divisions. For more than 45 years, we have been one of the leading service providers for Ports Management, Contract Logistics, Warehousing, Equipment Leasing, and Power Rental.

“We focus on the equipment and services, so you can focus on your project execution”
Our History

Milestones

1979 — Founded as an inland transportation company in Kuwait

Acquired Ports Services license for Shuwaikh Port

Launched Equipment Rental in Kuwait

Introduced Equipment Rental Operations in Qatar

Launched Power Rental in Kuwait

Established a formal subsidiary in Qatar — Road Junction Transport & Equipment Co. (RJTC)

Established a formal subsidiary in KSA — JTC Logistics Co.

Expanded Heavy Lift Capabilities

60% stake acquired by KIPCO Group

Commenced operations in Shuaiba Port for aggregate and general cargo stevedoring

Listed on the Kuwait Stock Exchange

Commissioned 10K temperature-controlled warehouses in Mina Abdullah

Opened Riyadh Depot for KSA Power Rental

Legal name changed to JTC Logistics Transportation & Stevedoring Co. K.S.C.P.; commissioned additional 10K & 12K temperature-controlled warehouses in Mina Abdullah

Opened Jeddah Depot for KSA Power Rental

Our Strategic Investor

Backed by QPIC

QPIC is one of the biggest private investors in the petrochemicals sector in Kuwait with total assets of around US$2.6 billion with a prime focus on the Energy and Industrial sectors. QPIC is part of the Kuwait Investment Projects Company (KIPCO) group of companies. KIPCO Group is one of the biggest holding companies in the Middle East and North Africa, with consolidated assets of US$ 34 billion as of 31 December 2019.
