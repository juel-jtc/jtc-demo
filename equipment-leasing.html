Overview

Equipment-on-demand
for your project

JTC's Equipment Leasing division provides equipment-on-demand for your project execution that supports clients across major sectors in Kuwait and Qatar. Owning and operating an extensive fleet of cranes, heavy lift equipment, and other industrial equipment helps JTC provides greater safety and efficiency to any heavy lifting or transport challenge. We offer a wide selection of services from daily rentals to customized project management packages.

IC-50 Ranked

Cranes

A globally ranked crane rental company (IC-50 list), we offer a wide range of capacities in the following crane variants:

  • All Terrain Cranes (100 - 700 Tons)
  • Rough Terrain Cranes (80 Tons)
  • Truck Mounted Cranes (25 - 80 Tons)
  • Crawler Cranes (150 - 650 Tons)
Heavy Lift

Heavy Lift

JTC offers state-of-the-art equipment and technical expertise to carry out operations safely and efficiently. From lift planning to project management, our capabilities include:

  • Demag AC 700-9: lift capacity up to 700T for individual cranes
  • Demag CC 3800-1: lift capacity up to 650T for crawler cranes at 12M radius
  • Prime Movers: 8x6 & 6x4 heavy duty heads
  • Modular Trailers: 54 axle lines up to 30 tons/axle
Material Handling

Material Handling Equipment

  • Mast Forklifts - From 2 to 15 Tons
